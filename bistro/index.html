Inside the Bistro

A private event venue in Redlands, designed to be beautiful and built to be flexible.

Warm wood, customizable ambient lighting, a beautiful full-service bar, a modest stage and integrated audio-visual setup. The Bistro reads like an intimate, high-end private dining room — not a cold rental hall. It provides the rare intersection of upscale atmosphere and absolute hosting freedom. The space comfortably accommodates up to 60 seated guests, or 90 for a standing reception.

Team dinners, business entertainment, and private meetings for companies across the Inland Empire — hosted comfortably and catered reliably.

Private tasting menus

A chef, a room of guests, and a menu coursed out of the kitchen next door and served immediately.

Specialized & wellness gatherings

Custom wellness dinners, plant-based menus, and elegant dry events. The Bistro provides an inclusive, accommodating setting that welcomes specialized menus, creative non-alcoholic pairings, and mindful hospitality.

Before you ask

The questions everyone has — answered plainly.

Is outside catering allowed?

Always. The Bistro is an open-vendor venue — the food is entirely your call.

Do you require in-house catering or charge a per-head fee?

No. The room is one flat, upfront price with no headcount surcharge — the number you see is the number you pay.

Can I cook or warm food on site?

Yes. A licensed commercial kitchen alongside the room is available by the hour if you or your caterer want to cook or finish on site.

Do I have to use your caterer?

No. Bring your own food, hire a caterer from the building, or build a menu from the community of makers who work here — three doors, your choice. Nobody is forced through one.

Can I bring my own food and drinks?

Yes. The room is yours; what's served in it is your call. If you or your caterer want to cook on site, a licensed commercial kitchen next door is available by the hour.

Is the price actually the price?

Yes. The room, the event-day kitchen, the event fee and the refundable deposit are published above, and your written brief itemizes anything you add. There is nothing to discover afterward.

How many people fit?

Up to 60 seated, or 90 for a standing reception. Tell us the format and the count on the form and we'll say plainly what works for your layout.

What does the event fee cover?

The utilities for your event, all the dishwashing, the clean-up and reset of the room, and the full service-ware — plates and tableware, glasses and stemware, chafing dishes and serving pieces, hotel and sheet pans, serving utensils. Nothing to rent, truck in, or return.

Can I see it first?

Yes. Ask on the form and we'll arrange a walkthrough — the room, the kitchens beside it, and the doors your food can come through.
