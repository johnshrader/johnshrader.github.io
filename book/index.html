Client reservations

Booking has moved to your client page.

From October 1, 2026, kitchen time, the meeting room and equipment are booked on your own Cooking Block client page — the same page that shows your account and the Board. The old booking site is closed. Bookings you already made are on your page under My bookings.

Step 1 · once on each phone or computer

Sign in — no password

Tap Client sign-in, enter the email we have on file for you, and we'll email you a sign-in link. Tap the link and you're in. You stay signed in on that phone or computer.

Step 2 · every booking

Book

On your page, tap Book, choose the kitchen, the meeting room or the equipment, then the day and the time. Pay by Zelle, bank transfer or card — your booking is confirmed by email.
