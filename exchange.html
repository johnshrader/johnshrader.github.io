The Exchange

Customers, meet your producer.

Dozens of food businesses work from Cooking Block's kitchens — caterers, bakers, charcuterie makers, specialty producers of every kind. The Exchange connects you with them directly: tell us what you're looking for, and we'll match you with the right business.

How it works

Three steps to the right producer.

1

Tell us what you need

A caterer for seventy, a custom cake, weekly office lunches, artisan bread for your restaurant — describe it in the form below.

2

We match you

We know every business in our kitchens — what they make, what they're great at, and who has capacity. We route your inquiry to the right fit.

3

They deliver

The producer contacts you directly, and you work together from there. Every one operates from a Health Department approved commercial kitchen.
