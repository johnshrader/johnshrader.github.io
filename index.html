The exclusive-use model

The whole kitchen. To yourself. Every booking.

Most shared kitchens rent you a table in a room full of strangers. Cooking Block rents you exclusive kitchen space to operate in freedom — every burner, every oven, every inch of 20+ linear feet of prep table — with nobody else in it.

  • Three full kitchens, bookable around the clock, 365 days a year
  • Access 24/7 — your own security code — opens for you and no one else
  • Dry and cold storage options so your ingredients live where you work
  • $25/hour since 2012 — no tiers, no peak pricing

Location
