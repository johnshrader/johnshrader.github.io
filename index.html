The exclusive-use model

The whole kitchen. To yourself. Every booking.

Most shared kitchens rent you a table in a room full of strangers. Cooking Block rents you an entire Health Department approved commercial kitchen — every burner, every oven, every inch of a minimum 18 linear feet of prep table — with nobody else in it.

  • Three full kitchens, each bookable around the clock, 365 days a year
  • Fob-secured access — come in at 2 PM or 2 AM, your schedule is the schedule
  • Dry and cold storage options so your ingredients live where you work
  • One flat $25/hour — no tiers, no peak pricing, no surprises
