Privacy

What we collect, and what we do with it.

Plainly, in one page. Effective 26 September 2026.

Cooking Block, LLC runs this website, cookingblock.com, and the client platform at app.cookingblock.com.

What we collect

What you tell us. When you fill in a form — the Fit Analysis, a Bistro inquiry, or a request for your sign-in link — we keep what you give us: your name, email, phone number and business, and what you tell us about your plans or your event.

If you become a client, what we need to run your account: your kitchen agreement, insurance certificate, bookings, invoices and payments, and the messages we exchange.

Visit counts. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without identifying you.

How we use it

To answer you, see whether we're a good fit, arrange tours and orientation, and — for clients — run bookings, billing and facility access, and keep the facility secure. Never for advertising.

Who else sees it

We never sell or rent your information. We share it only with the services that run our business for us — our email, accounting, payment and hosting providers — and only as far as that work needs, or when the law requires it.

Cookies

This website doesn't use cookies to track you. The client platform uses a sign-in cookie to keep you signed in, and nothing else.

Do Not Track

Because we don't track visitors across websites, a browser's "Do Not Track" signal changes nothing here — there's nothing to turn off.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email info@cookingblock.com. For clients, some records — agreements, invoices and payments — are kept as long as the law requires.

Children

This website isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this page changes, the date above changes with it. If a change matters to clients, we'll tell them by email.
