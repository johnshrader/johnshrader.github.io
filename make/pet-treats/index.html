The guide, not the rulebook

Where to make dog treats to sell in California.

If you bake, dehydrate or freeze-dry treats to sell, you're a pet food processor — and pet food is not "commercial feed," and it is not restaurant food. That distinction is why the county health department will wave you off, and why your path runs through a different door.

Baked, dried or freeze-dried treats

As we understand it, processed treats fall under the California Department of Public Health, Food and Drug Branch — a pet food processor licence tied to the facility you produce in. That facility has to be a licensed one. Confirm with CDPH what your specific product needs.

Raw or frozen pet food

Raw and frozen products are a different lane — as we understand it, the California Department of Food and Agriculture handles those. If your line is raw, start there and confirm.

Not the county health permit

The county's retail-food permit is for food people eat. Pet-treat makers are routinely told "we don't license that" — that's correct, and it's the sign you're in the right place here: a licensed commercial facility with a professional address, without the county's retail-food process.

Questions makers ask

Plain answers, then the door.

Do I really need a licensed facility for dog treats?

For baked, dried and freeze-dried treats sold in California, as we understand it, yes — the processor licence is tied to a licensed facility. Confirm with CDPH's Food and Drug Branch for your product; they're the authority, not us.

Will the county health department license me?

Usually not — pet food isn't their program, and they'll say so. That isn't a dead end. It's the reason the agencies above exist, and the reason a facility like this one is the practical answer.

What does it cost, and is there a contract?

$25 an hour. Time, utilities, equipment, cleaning chemicals and dry storage are included. The details are on the Become a Client page.

Can I keep my own equipment here?

Yes — dehydrators, freeze-dryers and packaging gear can live in your storage. Tell us what you run and we'll say where it fits.

Does my product contain hemp or CBD?

Then we're not the place — we're not set up for products containing hemp or CBD, and we'd rather tell you now than after a tour.

A note on all of this

This is what we understand about products like yours — general information, not legal advice, and never a substitute for the agency's own answer. Your product may have an exception we haven't seen. Confirm with the agency named above; we've written this so you're not starting from zero.
