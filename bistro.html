The room

Elegant enough for the toast. Equipped enough for the kitchen.

The Bistro seats intimate dinners and hosts standing receptions with equal ease. What no banquet hall can offer: a working commercial kitchen a few steps from the room — no ferrying trays across parking lots, no cold entrées, no compromises.
