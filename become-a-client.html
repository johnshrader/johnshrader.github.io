The health permit

We’ve made getting your health license easy.

Our streamlined arrangement with the Department of Health takes the worry away. The facility is Health Department approved, so your permit rides on our infrastructure, not on anything you have to build, buy, or engineer.

Even better: the Department of Health has assigned a dedicated agent to our facility. A friendly agent who knows this facility inside and out will meet you for your personal one-on-one orientation, right here at Cooking Block.

Your license is issued the same day. We’ve helped hundreds of food businesses get their start — and helped them grow — for over fourteen years.
